# Candy

Candy is one of the app's five styles: bright, soft and glossy. Cards are gradients in pink, violet
and blue, everything is round, what needs you sits in a big glossy orb, and Auto is a glossy little
robot who floats and blinks. It is the phone's side of the Candy theme on the web.

## Turn it on

Either of these:

- **Pick it.** Open **Settings › Style and look** and tap **Candy** ("Bright, glossy and round").
- **Follow the web.** Leave **Same as Studio** on (the default). When Candy is your theme in
  Automatos on the web, the phone switches to Candy by itself.

To leave Candy, pick another style there.

## The four views

A floating tab bar at the foot switches between Today, Shelf and Team. **Hold to talk** is the raised
button in its middle: hold it, say what you need, and let go. The **Chat** view has no tab bar: its own
message box and voice button sit at the foot, and the back circle at its top returns to Today.

The round **menu** button at the top of each view shows how many things need you. It opens New chat,
Chats, the Command Centre and Settings.

### Today

- A greeting for the time of day, and how many things need you ("3 things need you", "Nothing needs
  you").
- Auto at the top, and the orb with the number of things that need you.
- Three cards: **Working now** (tickets in progress), **Needs you** and **In queue** (tickets waiting
  to start). A card stays hidden until the app has its number.
- **Needs you**: the first two things waiting for you. Tap one to open it.
- **Ideas · N**, where your server offers Auto's suggestions: tap it to see them all.

### Chat

- Auto on his violet stage, with what he is doing in a pill ("● Listening", "Thinking") and **Pause**.
- The conversation, with "Auto is an AI" as its first line. Over each of Auto's replies, a chip for
  each thing he did: ✓ done, ● still running, ✕ failed. Anything he made appears as a row you can
  open. Cards that need your answer appear in the conversation, and an approval opens its sheet by
  itself.
- The day's brief, in Auto's reply, where your server offers it.
- Auto's newest open suggestion, pinned just above the message box.
- **Message Auto**, with the voice button beside it.
- When an approval is waiting and you closed its sheet without answering, **◆ WAITING FOR YOU ·
  OPEN** sits under Auto's state. Tap it to answer.

### Shelf

Your Deliverables as gradient tiles, two to a row. The chips at the top (**All**,
**Reports**, **Documents**, **Images**, **Spreadsheets**, **Slides**) show one kind at a time.
**More** loads more, and **All Deliverables** opens them in the Command Centre. An empty shelf shows
Auto asleep and a way to the chat.

### Team

"Your team", how many of your agents are active, a row of up to five avatars (the active ones first,
ringed), then every agent with its status: **● Needs you**, **● Working** or **○ Ready**.

## Make it sweet: your look

**Settings › Style and look › Make it sweet →** is Candy's look page. Pick a preset, then tune it;
the preview changes as you go.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | **Blush** (pink on white, the default), **Lilac** (violet into blue) or **Night bloom** (neon pinks after dark). Night bloom is dark and the others light, whatever your phone's Light or Dark setting. |
| **Card colours** | **Mixed**: the cards take pink, violet and blue in turn. **One colour**: all take the first. |
| **Gradient** | **Soft**, **Rich** or **Vivid**. Vivid adds a soft glow behind the home. |
| **Roundness** | A slider from 16 to 40: how round the cards and windows are. |
| **Glow** | 0 to 10: how strong the coloured shadows are. |
| **Motion** | **On** (Auto floats and blinks) or **Paused** (everything still). |

**Save look** keeps it as one of your own looks ("My look 1", …), listed under **Your looks**.
**Reset** goes back to the preset. Touch and hold one of your looks to rename or delete it; presets
can't be renamed or deleted.

Your looks are the same ones you have on the web while Candy is installed in your workspace: change
one on either and the other follows. If Candy isn't installed there, the page says "Candy isn't
installed in this workspace, so your looks stay on this phone."

## Stopping the motion

Tap **Pause** (it becomes **Play**) on any view, on the Chat stage, on the voice screen or on Make
it sweet to still everything at once: Auto, the orb's ring and the writing dots. The phone remembers
it. With **Reduce Motion** on in your iPhone's settings, or Motion set to Paused in your look,
nothing moves and there is no Pause button.

## On an older version of the app

A version of the app from before Candy draws its usual Studio screens in Candy's colours and fonts,
with Auto's pictures. Update the app to get the Candy views.
