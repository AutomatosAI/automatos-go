# Arcade

Arcade is one of the app's built-in styles: Automatos as a 16-bit role-playing game.
- Your agents are your party.
- Auto is the guide in a dialogue box, drawn as a small pixel robot.
- Your Board's work is quests on a map.
- Your Deliverables are items in your Inventory.

It's the phone's version of Arcade on the web (`automatos/arcade`), with the same palettes and the same settings.

## Choosing Arcade

- **Pick it yourself:** go to **Settings › Style and look**, then **Arcade**.
- **Follow the web:** with **Same as Studio** chosen there, the phone follows the theme Studio shows you. When Arcade
  is installed and active in Studio, the phone switches to Arcade by itself.

## The home

The home is a handheld console. From top to bottom:

**The status bar** shows two counters:
- **!**: what needs you;
- **QUEST**: how many tickets on your Board are in progress.

Arcade on the web also shows XP, the tasks done today. The phone leaves it out, because the Board doesn't record when a
ticket was finished.

**The party** lists your agents, each with what it's doing:
- **! Needs you**: something of theirs is waiting for you;
- **● Working**: one of their tickets is in progress;
- **Ready**: neither.

**The story** is your conversation:
- **YOU** lines are yours, and **AUTO** lines are Auto's.
- A **★** line marks something a turn did.
- An **ITEM RECEIVED!** window appears for each Deliverable Auto makes, with **Open**.
- Approvals wait under **! A CHOICE AWAITS**.
- The small "Auto is an AI" caption opens every conversation.

**The dialogue box** holds:
- Auto's portrait and a word for what he's doing (thinking, working, searching…);
- his newest reply, typing out at your text speed (**Skip** shows it whole);
- **Type**, which opens the message box, and **Stop** while he's answering;
- **Report this**: touch and hold the reply.

**The controls** are a D-pad with four named buttons, and the voice button in a round surround (hold to talk):
- **▲ Quests** shows the map;
- **◀ Chat** goes back to the conversation;
- **▶ Inventory** shows your Deliverables;
- **▼ Menu** opens the menu: New chat, Chats, Command Centre and Settings.

## Quests

Your Board's tickets are quests on an overworld map, in three regions:
- **Greenwood**: tickets in progress;
- **Storm Peaks**: tickets that need you;
- **Hearth Village**: finished tickets.

Touch a marker to see its quest under the map. **Open** opens the ticket, and **Board** opens the whole Board. With no
tickets, the map says it's quiet.

## Inventory

Your Deliverables appear as item slots, newest first, with tabs for **All**, **Reports**, **Documents** and **Images**
(and **Posts** where your workspace has Socials).
- The selected slot has a ▶ cursor, and a line under the grid names it.
- **Open** shows the Deliverable, and a link opens all your Deliverables.
- With none yet, Auto sleeps in an empty bag.

## Auto and PAUSE

Auto is a 32-pixel robot with twelve states, from hello and thinking to celebrate and sleeping. His state follows the
conversation.

**PAUSE** stops every moving thing: Auto, the ▶ cursor and the typing. **PLAY** starts them again. You'll find it
wherever Arcade moves: on the home, the voice screen, Options, and the screens the app shows when it can't reach your
server.

If your phone is set to **Reduce Motion**, nothing moves and replies show whole.

## Options

Options is Arcade's look page: **Settings › Style and look**, then **Options** under Arcade. It has five settings:

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| Palette | Overworld, Dungeon, Neon Cabinet, Night City, Handheld |
| Scanlines | Off, Soft, Strong |
| Pixel scale | 2×, 3× |
| Window | Classic double, Thin |
| Text speed | Instant, Normal, Slow |

- **Live preview:** a preview shows your changes as you make them.
- **Saving:** **Save look** keeps the look in your save slots, beside the five ready-made ones; touch and hold a slot
  to rename or delete it. **Reset** goes back to the palette as it ships.
- **Sync:** while Arcade is installed in your workspace, your looks are the same on the phone and on the web. If it
  isn't installed, they stay on this phone, and Options says so.

## Everywhere else

- **Other screens:** the Command Centre, its sheets, Settings and the voice screen appear in Arcade's square windows,
  pixel type and palette. On the voice screen, Auto sits in the middle as the pixel robot.
- **Status colours:** a status shows as a symbol and a word, in its colour:

  | Status | Colour |
  |---|---|
  | needs you | yellow |
  | working | blue |
  | done | plain text |
  | failed | red |

## Auto's brief, ideas and approvals

**These need your server to offer them:**
- **The day's brief:** when it's the newest message, the dialogue box says it, with **Open brief** to see all of it.
  A brief that isn't the newest message stays in the story as a window, headed **★ Today's brief** or with its
  date.
- **Auto's ideas:** an **Ideas** count and a **▶ Next** line appear under the status bar, with **Start** and
  **Hide**. A suggestion Auto posts in the conversation shows as a card in the story.

**These work as usual:**
- **Approvals:** when something waits for your answer, the approval opens by itself. If you close it, a line under the
  dialogue box opens it again.
- **Opening at a message:** when the app opens a conversation at one message, such as the brief, the story scrolls to
  it.

## Large text

Arcade wraps at every text size, up to the largest accessibility sizes. The D-pad's buttons grow to at most a third of
the screen's width, and their words wrap.
